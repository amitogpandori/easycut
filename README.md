# EasyCut

An easy, InShot-style video editor for Windows, made for vloggers and reel makers.

## Features
- **Project menu** (📁 Project): New project (Ctrl+N), Open (Ctrl+O), recent projects, Save (Ctrl+S), Save as (Ctrl+Shift+S), Rename, Export (Ctrl+E), Project info, Remove unused media, Optimize heavy videos, Close project (Ctrl+W). Asks to save unsaved changes before closing or starting a new project
- **Reel Maker songs**: add several songs at once; EasyCut counts them, shows the total music time and asks whether to use **all songs one after another** (cuts follow each song's own beat, video matches the songs) or **only one**. Reorder or remove songs. Length option "Match the songs"
- **How long each clip should be**: follow the beat, 1 / 2 / 3 / 5 / 8 s or type any length (kept on the beat when there's a song)
- **Effects amount**: Light (default) / Medium / Strong / None
- Music never jumps back during playback (the song keeps the time)
- **Wildlife & nature pack (1.15)**: 11 Reel Maker styles built from how wildlife creators describe their edits: 🐾 Wildlife Documentary (calm cuts, light grade, white lower-third captions), 🦅 Bird in Flight (speed ramps that slow on the beat), 🌅 Golden Hour, 👁️ Zoom Into the Eye, 🦁 Safari Diaries (binocular open, warm highlights / cool shadows), 🔬 Macro World, 🌑 Silhouette, 📋 Field Notes (camera viewfinder + EXIF info cards), 🎚️ RAW → Edit (before/after wipe on every shot), 🐆 Predator Mode and 🌊 Ocean Blue. Plus 🎞️ Cinematic Travel and 🖼️ Polaroid Memories
- **Reel templates** (PC and phone): choose a whole-reel preset in **3 · Choose a reel template**, or let Auto choose. New original styles—Keepsake Scrapbook, Pixel Pop, Slow Burn, Bold Editorial and Wonderstruck—adapt the 2027 scrapbook, expressive-tech, handcrafted, bold-type and dreamy design directions ([Canva Design Trends](https://www.canva.com/design-trends/)). Each preset includes its own title, grade, motion, transitions and effects.
- **Smarter Reel Maker**: 🪄 **Auto** looks at your photos and videos and picks a style (it tells you why, e.g. "greens and earth tones, looks like nature or wildlife"). **Smart picks** scores every shot for sharpness, exposure, colour and how clear the subject is, skips near-duplicate burst shots, keeps the best part of each video and puts your best shot at the song's high point. **Story order** (default) opens wide, builds up, saves the hero shot for the peak and ends calm. Cuts and transitions follow the song's **energy** (fast in loud parts, soft in quiet parts). **Camera info captions** read the EXIF of your photos (camera, lens, focal length, f-number, shutter, ISO, date, GPS). **🎞️ 3 creative versions** keeps your chosen style for A, then builds B/C with contrasting looks and three different shot orders—not just a new random seed—so you can compare a narrative arc, shuffled cut and original order. Inspired by [photographers’ story-first image curation](https://photographylife.com/storytelling-through-image-culling), [editors’ notes on rhythm, surprise and emotional pauses](https://blog.frame.io/2022/03/23/award-worthy-editing/) and [editing with silence](https://blog.frame.io/2017/05/08/edit-with-silence/)
- **Wildlife templates**: Species Name Card, Camera Settings (filled from EXIF), Freeze + Info Card, Viewfinder POV, Binocular Spotting, Zoom Into the Eye, Slow Reveal, RAW vs Edit, Location & Date, Golden Hour Title, Documentary Opener, Conservation End Card, Behind the Shot. **Looks** (one tap for the whole video): Documentary, Golden Hour, Safari, Silhouette, Arctic, Deep Jungle, Crisp Detail, Cinematic Teal, Polaroid. New effects: camera viewfinder, binoculars, focus pull, polaroid frame, RAW → edit wipe, sun flare. New camera moves: zoom to subject and slow reveal. All of them work on photos and videos
- **Phone (Android) app, closer to the PC version**: projects save on the phone (📁 Project → Save / Open / recent, with all media inside), **⋯ Pro** row on the timeline (jump to cut, frame step, markers, In/Out, start/end, fit), pinch to zoom the timeline, ↑ ↓ to reorder clips in Reel Maker, and every panel, the clip settings (motion, keyframes, colour) and export options as touch sheets. Still PC only: the AI Editor (needs Ollama), AI upscaling and "Optimize heavy videos" (need FFmpeg / Real-ESRGAN), keyboard shortcuts, and saving .easycut files to disk
- **Smoother on phones (1.15)**: swipe the timeline anywhere, even starting on a clip (a tap selects, so swipes no longer open clip settings by accident). **Long-press** a clip to lift and drag it, with a small vibration, auto-scroll at the edges and snapping. Bigger trim handles on the selected clip. Pinch zoom draws once per frame. After a swipe, the playhead settles on a nearby cut. Sheets slide up with a spring and close with a **swipe down** on their top bar. Clip settings sit above the timeline so you can still trim. Works sideways too (preview on the left, timeline on the right). While you scrub, the preview draws at lower resolution and sharpens when your finger rests. On slower phones playback draws at most 30 preview frames a second. Thumbnails are cached and load lazily. No accidental page zoom, and safe-area insets are respected. Export quality is unchanged
- **Photo formats (PC and phone)**: import JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF, HEIC/HEIF, AVIF (where the device WebView supports it), and TIFF/TIF (converted to JPEG for editing). Standard browser-readable photos are resized once at high quality for smooth previews; TIFF uses its embedded first image. DSLR RAW formats (such as CR2/CR3, NEF, ARW and DNG) are not supported yet—convert them to JPG or TIFF first. Photos work in regular editing, Reel Maker and the photo editor, alone or mixed with videos; orientation metadata is honored where available.
- **Photo reels (1.14)**: photos work everywhere videos do, alone or mixed with videos. Very large photos are resized once at high quality so they stay sharp and the preview stays smooth. Photos export at full quality (1080 × 1920 and up). Set one length for every photo in one tap. **12 camera moves** (Ken Burns): zoom in/out, pan in four directions, zoom-and-drift, diagonal and snap zoom, eased so they glide. Reel Maker mixes the moves so no two photos in a row move the same way. It also offers **Smart framing** (tall photos fill the screen, wide ones show whole on a blurred background), **time on screen for each photo** (on the beat, or 1–5 s) and **captions on any photo or clip**
- **Trend styles for Reel Maker**: 📸 Photo Dump (fast beat cuts, flash and swipe, film look, glowing date stamp), 🌀 Velocity Edit (speed ramps on video, snap zooms on photos), 🤍 Minimal Clean, 👾 Glitch Core, 🎞️ Film Roll (film burn and light leak transitions, grain) and 👉 Swipe Carousel
- **Speed ramps (velocity)** on any video clip: Velocity (slow → fast → slow), Fast → slow-mo, and Slow → fast. The clip keeps its length
- **11 new transitions (27 in total)**: Zoom blur, Whip up, Swipe, Light leak, Film burn, RGB split, Shake, Clock wipe, Diamond, Blinds, Doors
- **New effects**: Film burn, Light leak sweep, Light sweep, Date stamp, Motion blur, Chromatic aberration, Teal & orange grade. **New filters**: Teal & Orange, Film, Moody, Y2K, Bleach bypass, Pastel
- **New text animations**: Word by word (caption style), Bounce, Zoom in, Blur in. **8 new templates**: Photo Dump, Year Recap, POV, Wait for the drop, Word-by-word caption, Blur-in title, Lyric line, Swipe for more
- **Smoother editing** (PC and phone): scrubbing, dragging and trimming on the timeline update once per screen frame, the video jumps straight to where you scrub instead of queueing up every frame in between, the playhead glides on the graphics card, zooming with Ctrl+scroll or the slider no longer stutters, only the timeline tracks that changed are redrawn, and on Android taps react instantly and panels slide in
- **Add many songs at once** (Music): EasyCut asks whether they play one after another or all together (layered), and can stop the music with a fade when the video ends
- **Reel Maker 2.0**: shows how much footage you have (e.g. "7:45 of video + 2 photos"), lets you pick 15s / 30s / 1 min / 1:30 / 3 min / all footage or type any length (45, 2:30, 1:05:00). Picks different moments from across every clip, **never uses the same footage twice**, and shuffles them (or keeps them in order). 🎲 New mix makes a fresh version. Effects: the style's own, **shuffled by video type** (Kids, Party, Cinematic, Energetic, Retro, Love, Calm, Travel) or **picked by you**, on some or every cut. 22 styles including new Kids Cartoon, Birthday, Party Night, Retro VHS, Love Story, Nature Calm, Sports Highlights, Festival/Diwali, Gurpurab/Punjabi and Unboxing
- **Smooth playback**: a second player per video so cuts between different parts of the same video are ready in advance; **⚡ Optimize** (Windows) makes edit-friendly copies of heavy phone/4K/HDR videos (1080p, 30 fps, keyframe every ½ s, iPhone HDR to normal colors) — offered on import, in Media (Optimize all), in Reel Maker, and automatically when playback keeps freezing; **Preview quality** Full / Half / Third in Canvas; uses the dedicated NVIDIA/AMD GPU on dual-graphics laptops
- **59 templates** (16 new: kids intro/outro, episode number, birthday, Diwali, Gurpurab, Top 5 countdown, before/after, location tag, quote card, coming up next, giveaway, upload schedule, Hindi/Punjabi like & subscribe, subscribe end screen)
- **AI Upscale (Windows app, free)**: select a video clip → 🔍 AI Upscale → choose 1080p, 2K or 4K and the type of video. *Cartoon / AI video* uses Real-ESRGAN AnimeVideo-v3 (fast, great for animation and AI-generated clips), *Real camera* uses Real-ESRGAN x4plus (stronger, slower), *Quick* uses FFmpeg Lanczos + denoise + sharpening in seconds. Runs on your own graphics card through Vulkan (GTX 1650 and up); the tools (~100 MB) download once on first use. If the card can't run the AI, a standard upscale is used automatically. The sharp copy replaces the clip in your project and is saved in Videos\\EasyCut
- **✨ Enhance** (PC and phone): one tap sharpens a clip and adds clarity and richer color; **export in 720p, 1080p, 2K or 4K**
- **Batch Auto Color** (PC and phone): in Media, balance exposure, contrast, warmth and vibrance across every photo and video on the timeline in one tap; undo once to restore the whole edit
- **AI Editor (Windows app, free and offline)**: type what you want — "remove the pauses, add fades, a title and a cinematic look" — and a free AI model running on your own PC through [Ollama](https://ollama.com) edits the timeline for you. EasyCut finds Ollama, starts it, and downloads a model with one click (Qwen 3.5 2B recommended for 4 GB graphics cards; Llama 3.2 3B and Qwen 2.5 3B as options). Every AI change shows a list of what it did and can be undone in one tap. It can split, trim, reorder, shorten to a length, change speed/volume, add transitions, looks, color, filters, motion, text, templates, effects, change the canvas, remove silences, cut at scene changes, adjust music and make a reel
- **Pro editing (Premiere-style)**: keyframes for position, scale, rotation, opacity and volume (with easing); Lumetri-style color grading on the graphics card (exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, whites, blacks, temperature, tint, vibrance, saturation, fade, sharpen) with 10 looks, auto color, copy/paste grade and your own .cube LUTs; blend modes for overlays; markers (M); In/Out points (I/O, X) with export of just that range; jump between edits (↑/↓); J/K/L playback; slip edit (Alt+drag); mute/hide tracks; snapping on/off; audio level meter; shared export settings for every edit (720p–4K, Standard/High/Maximum quality). Maximum quality is the default and is saved with each project.
- **Smart cuts**: remove silent parts from talking clips automatically, and cut a clip at every scene change
- **Templates throughout editing**: reusable look recipes live in Filters, layered effect recipes in Effects, and smooth transition styles plus scene detection in Transitions; layouts, title cards, text styles and Reel Maker templates stay in their matching tools
- **Scene-aware transitions**: scene detection samples video frames for isolated hard cuts, splits the clip at likely scene boundaries, then applies eased cross-dissolves. Fast camera movement, flashes, gradual dissolves and very dark footage can still affect automatic detection; review the cuts before export
- **Automatic updates** (1.16.0+): Windows downloads new versions in the background and installs them when you close EasyCut; Android shows what's new and installs the new APK from inside the app
- **Android app** (APK) with a phone layout: preview, timeline with a centred playhead you swipe, bottom toolbar, tools and clip settings slide up as sheets; videos save to Documents/EasyCut with a share sheet (Photos, WhatsApp, Instagram…); back button works

## Download
**Android:** on your phone open the latest Release and download `EasyCut-x.y.z.apk`, open it and tap Install (allow installing from your browser/Files the first time). It's signed with the same key every time, so new versions install over the old one.

**Windows:** Go to **Releases** and download `EasyCut-Setup-x.y.z.exe`, then run it.
Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't code-signed yet:
click **More info → Run anyway**.

### Updates (1.16.0 and newer)
- **Windows:** EasyCut checks GitHub Releases when it opens and every 4 hours, downloads a new version in the background and shows a small banner: **Restart now**, or just close EasyCut and it installs itself.
- **Android:** EasyCut asks GitHub for the latest release when it opens (at most once an hour) and when you come back to it (at most every 6 hours). If there's a newer version it shows what's new with an **Update** button, downloads the APK and opens Android's installer, where you tap **Update**. The first time, Android asks you to let EasyCut install apps (**Allow from this source**). If that can't work, it opens the download in your browser.
- Both: 📁 Project → **Check for updates** any time, and **Check automatically** to switch the automatic checks off or on. 1.15.0 and older can't update themselves: install 1.16.0 by hand once.

## Develop
```
npm install
npm start      # run the app
npm test       # automated end-to-end test of the desktop app
npm run dist   # build the Windows installer into dist/
```

GitHub Actions tests the app, builds the installer and publishes it as a Release on every push to `main`.
Bump `version` in package.json for each new release.
